Refund amount

If the purchase was at a purchasing-power discount, the amount is what was paid. Otherwise it is the course's current list price.

Where the code disagrees with itself

  • The 50% rule is only in the interface. A learner past 50% has no button, but a direct post to the route would still be accepted.
  • Amounts can differ between screens. The form and email use the rule above, and Settings shows the amount paid. A three-seat purchase at $79 per seat, with no seats claimed, shows $79 on the form and $237 on Settings.
  • The promise outruns the code. The form says money goes back to the card. No code does that.
  • A resolved request would not block a new one. The open-request check only looks for requested.
