# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: User

**Support Team Member**:
Someone at Cadence who decides Refund Requests. Together they are the Support Team.
_Avoid_: Staff, Team, Team Member (a Team is a customer organisation that buys seats)

### Seats

**Seat**:
One person's place on a course, bought as part of a team purchase and handed out as a code.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat whose code has been redeemed. It can't be refunded.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The Support Team approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen days after a purchase in which a Learner can raise a Refund Request. Only the day it was raised counts, so time spent waiting for a decision never counts against the Learner.

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the Support Team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the Support Team has accepted, which is final. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and no further Refund Request can be raised on that purchase.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the Support Team has turned down, which is final. It has no Refund, and the Learner may raise a new Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Denied

**Reason for Decline**:
The Support Team's explanation to the Learner of why a Refund Request was declined. It is separate from the reason the Learner gave when raising the Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Decline Reason, Denial reason

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is the full amount they paid, less the price of any Claimed Seats. A Declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the Support Team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the Learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
